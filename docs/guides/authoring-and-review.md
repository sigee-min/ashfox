# Create and refine

Describe the result you want in ordinary language. Include size, colors,
attachments, or motion when they matter. For an existing model, say what to
keep as well as what to change; the agent handles how to build it.

For example: “Make the head wider, but keep the expression and eye marks the
same size.”

## Check the result

Look at the model from several angles and at the size it will appear in your
game. Watch the animation for a full loop. Check that the shape reads clearly,
colors work together, and parts stay connected as they move.

If something looks wrong, describe what you see: “The shield floats away from
the hand during the turn.” Ask the agent to fix it and review the result again.
Passing validation alone does not mean the model looks right.

When you are happy with it, [save the workspace or export the
model](save-and-export.md).

For agents working with source, [Asset language](../architecture/asset-language.md)
and [Precision modeling](precision-modeling.md) provide syntax and examples.
